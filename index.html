Margin sets the fee.

Tokenized stocks trade all night; the stock market does not. An agent prices that gap on Uniswap v4 pools, raises the fee when arbitrageurs are coming and lowers it when they are not, and says why, every time, with numbers taken from chain.

How it works

01 · THE HOOK

An immutable Uniswap v4 hook accepts a signed fee for each pool, bounded between 0.05% and 2.00% and valid for at most fifteen minutes. No owner, no upgrade, no pause. If the agent goes quiet, the pool falls back to 0.30% on its own.

02 · THE AGENT

Every few minutes Margin reads the NYSE clock, the pool price against the Chainlink reference, recent flow, volatility and earnings, decides a fee, and signs it. Code clamps the number and rejects any reason that cites a figure not in the inputs.

03 · THE LEDGER

Each decision is published here and on-chain as events. The daily line is the honest one: what liquidity providers earned in fees and what they lost to informed flow, next to a plain 0.30% pool as the control.

What the chain says

Fourteen days of every swap on the live TSLA, AAPL and NVDA pools, measured by markout: did the price move against the pool in the half hour after each trade.
